Set up your phone

1. Install the app

Download the app

Open the downloaded file. Android asks to allow installs from your browser: allow it, then tap Install. What the app does and can access: android/README.md.

2. Pair the printer

Turn the printer on and pair it in your phone's Bluetooth settings. It shows up as S1- plus four characters.

3. Connect

Connect the app to this server

Opens the app and asks to connect to {{ server }}. Tap Connect, and Allow when it asks about nearby devices.
